## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for mission-critical B2B financial infrastructure, billing engines, and enterprise revenue operations. The interface balances ultra-high information density with effortless legibility, instilling institutional trust, audited accuracy, and modern technical sophistication.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Measured:** Every element projects stability, computational precision, and fiduciary responsibility. No frivolous ornamentation or distracting motion.
- **Data-Dense Yet Legible:** Interfaces optimize screen real estate for financial controllers, ops leads, and VP-level executives who scan thousands of records daily.
- **Architectural & Crisp:** Clean linear structures, hairline boundary definitions, and mathematical alignment create a calm, controlled analytical workspace.

### Design Movement: Precision Technical Modernism
A hybrid of high-utility Swiss modernist typography and modern enterprise engineering:
- **Hairstreak Outlines:** Clean 1px structural framing eliminates visual clutter while keeping dense numeric tables strictly partitioned.
- **Purposeful Functional Color:** Color is strictly semantic. Backgrounds and chrome are neutral slates and deep navy-infused charcoals, reserving saturated green, amber, and coral tones solely for metric state indications.
- **Micro-elevation:** Elevation relies on tonal shifts and ultra-subtle ambient occlusions rather than heavy drop shadows, maintaining focus on financial telemetry.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for complex financial records, auditing trails, and billing life cycles. Color usage adheres to strict semantic governance to eliminate misinterpretation of critical fiscal events.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Roles
- **Primary (`#1E293B` - Deep Slate Navy):** Anchors main interactive triggers, high-emphasis text, active states in primary navigation, and high-impact actions.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Applied to persistent navigation frames, top-level enterprise shells, sidebar containers, and multi-tenant boundary lines.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` - Deep Emerald / Vibrant Teal):** Used for forward financial progress, recognized revenue, successful reconciliations, active subscriptions, and positive growth deltas.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Grey):** De-emphasized secondary text, tabular metadata labels, structural grid borders, and non-interactive structural framing.

### Specialized Functional Semantic Tokens
- **Positive / Realized Revenue (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Net retention gains, paid invoices, collected revenues, and upward MRR trajectories.
- **Warning / Dunning & Pending (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** In grace period, pending settlements, upcoming card expirations, retry schedules, and dunning cycles.
- **Critical / Failed Charges & Churn (`#E11D48` / `#F43F5E`):** Failed webhooks, payment chargebacks, churn events, invoice voids, and delinquent accounts.
- **Surface Foundations:** Background canvas sits at `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50), with card containers at `#FFFFFF` (White) and nested secondary groupings at `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) to create clear tonal nesting.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is divided into three distinct functional tools:

1. **Hanken Grotesk (Structural Headings & Core KPIs):** Imparts modern authority without geometric distortion. Used for dashboard overviews, customer statements, and primary KPI cards.
2. **Inter (General Body, Tables, and Forms):** Delivers clean readability across dense multi-column arrays, billing descriptions, and ledger lines.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Financial Digits, UUIDs, and Ledger Values):** Applied strictly to tabular currency, timestamps, tax identification codes, and webhook payload logs. Tabular lining numbers (`tnum`) ensure absolute vertical alignment across transaction amounts.

### Typographic Rules for Financial Display
- Currency symbols must match the font family of their associated amount but render with an opacity of 70% or in a medium font weight to emphasize the numerical value.
- Decimal points and cents in tabular data views may drop to a secondary weight or 80% size when subordinate to whole dollar valuations.
- All column headers over numeric values must right-align in parity with the numbers below them.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a fluid 12-column responsive grid designed to hold dense dashboards, full-width data grids, split billing drawers, and analytical telemetry.

### Breakpoint Strategy & Layout Reflow
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin `1rem` (`16px`), Gutter `1rem`. Global side navigation collapses into a bottom action bar or drawer. Data tables revert to responsive vertical card lists. Metric grids drop to a 1-column stack.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Margin `1.5rem` (`24px`), Gutter `1rem`. Metric blocks arrange into 2x2 grids. The primary sidebar collapses into an icon-only rail (width `64px`).
- **Desktop (1025px - 1440px):** 12-column layout. Margin `2rem` (`32px`), Gutter `1.5rem` (`24px`). Full collapsible sidebar (`240px` wide). High-density split layouts (6-column ledger / 6-column invoice preview).
- **Wide Desktop (> 1440px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (`1600px` content container), centered with dynamic safe margins. High-density data tables take priority with horizontal pinned action rails.

### Spacing Discipline
- **Data Table Rows:** Compact row heights (`36px` to `44px`) utilizing `space-xs` and `space-sm` internal padding to maximize vertical visibility of transaction entries.
- **Component Separation:** Cards and metrics maintain a strict `space-lg` gap on desktop to prevent visual crowding despite high data density.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the focus on data precision, this design system rejects heavy, theatrical drop shadows. Elevation is conveyed through tonal layering, crisp hairline borders, and targeted ambient occlusions.

### Depth Hierarchy

1. **Base Layer (Canvas / Level 0):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational canvas backing all analytical workflows.
2. **Card Surfaces (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` bordered by a 1px solid stroke of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Used for metric cards, table containers, and filter modules.
3. **Hover & Interactive Tiers (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF` with border `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Applied to active cards, hovered ledger rows, and selected segment blocks.
4. **Contextual Flyouts, Menus & Multi-Tenant Pickers (Level 3):** `#FFFFFF` with border `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
5. **Overlays & Sliders (Drawers / Modals / Level 4):** Backdrop obscured by `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`. Sheet surface: `#FFFFFF` with a left-edge border of `#E2E8F0` and shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** corner geometry. Roundedness is intentionally constrained to convey architectural rigor, enterprise stability, and geometric compactness.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `rounded` = `0.25rem` (`4px`). Retains clean linear edges that sit harmoniously next to vertical table lines and grid axes.
- **Containers & Data Cards:** `rounded-lg` = `0.5rem` (`8px`). Creates a subtle distinction between individual modules while preventing excessive empty corner space.
- **Modals & Flyout Sheets:** `rounded-xl` = `0.75rem` (`12px`). Reserved exclusively for top-level floating overlays.
- **Pills / Status Dots:** Circular or fully rounded forms (`rounded-full`) are strictly restricted to state-indicator dots, micro counts, and user identity avatars.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1E293B` background, white text, 4px corner radius, height `36px` (compact) or `40px` (standard). Hover: `#0F172A`. Active: `#020617`.
- **Secondary Action:** White background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#1E293B` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with `#94A3B8` border.
- **Destructive Action:** Solid `#E11D48` background for irreversible financial actions (voiding transactions, cancelling subscriptions); secondary destructive utilizes an outline in `#F43F5E` on hover.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Structure:** Height `22px`, horizontal padding `8px`, border-radius `4px`, font `label-sm` (`11px` bold uppercase or title case).
- **Paid / Active:** Background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`. Includes a 6px solid emerald dot.
- **Pending / Dunning:** Background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`. Includes a 6px pulsing amber dot.
- **Failed / Churned:** Background `#FFF1F2`, border `#FECDD3`, text `#9F1239`. Includes a 6px coral dot.
- **Draft / Inactive:** Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#475569`.

### Data Tables (High-Density)
- **Header:** Height `36px`, background `#F8FAFC`, border-bottom `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `label-sm` in `#64748B`. Unambiguous sort indicators with persistent hover states.
- **Row:** Height `40px` (ultra-dense) or `48px` (default). Alternating row striping is omitted; instead, use a 1px bottom border of `#F1F5F9` and hover illumination in `#F8FAFC`.
- **Alignment:** Strings and descriptors left-align. Statuses center-align. Currency, numbers, and date tokens strictly right-align using `JetBrains Mono`.

### Metric Cards & Sparklines
- **Card Structure:** 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `16px` internal padding, white surface.
- **Header:** Metric label (`label-md`, `#64748B`) paired with a period badge (e.g., "vs Last Mo").
- **Body:** Large financial value (`metric-display`, `#0F172A`) paired with an inline indicator showing percentage change (emerald for positive, coral for negative).
- **Footer:** Integrated responsive SVG sparkline (`40px` height) with smooth curves; positive runs use `#0D9488` with a 10% vertical linear fade to transparent.

### Multi-Tenant Organization Switcher
- Compact navigation trigger displaying the tenant logo/monogram (`20px` square, `4px` radius), company name (`body-sm`, bold), and role badge (`code-badge`, e.g., `PROD: US-EAST`).
- Dropdown menu groups enterprise environments by status (Production, Staging, Sandbox) with distinctive color rails on each option item.

### Input Fields & Monetary Entry
- Height `36px`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Focus ring: `2px solid #0D9488` with zero shadow offset.
- Currency fields include an integrated, non-editable prefix block (`USD $`, `EUR €`) set in `#64748B` with a `#F1F5F9` background, visually joined to the input container.
